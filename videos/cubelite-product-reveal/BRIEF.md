## Intent

A 30-second motion-design product reveal for CubeLite, built from the live preview at
https://massimilianolapuma.github.io/cubelite/. It promotes the product; it is not a literal
tour of the page. Embedded in the author's portfolio and published on LinkedIn / YouTube.
Atmosphere: premium developer tool — dark, essential, trustworthy, terminal precision with
native-product quality. Large, highly legible text; sober, fluid, seek-safe motion. Every line
must work with the sound off.

Storyboard as given by the user (on-screen copy is verbatim):

1. 0–4 s — Hook: "Too many Kubernetes contexts?" Many fragmented contexts converge into one point.
2. 4–9 s — "One unified interface." A node system orders itself; CubeLite appears.
3. 9–15 s — "Discover. Switch. Manage." Three beats with clean transitions. Introduce multi-kubeconfig and quick-switch.
4. 15–21 s — "Local-first by design." Credentials stay inside the local perimeter; "No telemetry. No cloud account."
5. 21–26 s — "Native where you work." Native macOS and cross-platform desktop availability, without invented UI.
6. 26–30 s — Close: "CubeLite" and "Kubernetes contexts, without the friction." CTA: massimilianolapuma.github.io/cubelite

## Customizations

- Capture and analyze the site first; colors, typography and design tokens come from the site.
- Music: minimal, precise, discreet electronic bed (HeyGen catalog); clean fade-out. No voice-over.
- Export an H.264 MP4 optimized for the web (faststart) after the final render.

## Notes

- Claims must be verifiable from the preview, and only these: one-click context switching;
  multi-kubeconfig support; menu-bar quick-switch; credentials protected in the Keychain;
  no telemetry or cloud account; native macOS app in SwiftUI and cross-platform desktop with Tauri.
- Do NOT invent app screenshots (the preview says the screenshot is coming soon).
- Visualize clusters, kubeconfigs, contexts and credentials with nodes, abstract panels,
  connection lines and editorial graphics.
- Avoid: stock footage, generic datacenters, robots, falling code, hooded hackers, fake Kubernetes dashboards.
- Review flow requested by the user: storyboard first, then a preview; check every line is
  readable, transitions have no jumps, and the music fades well.
